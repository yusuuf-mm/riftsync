# Third-party assets

## Cesium Man

The animated, skinned human mesh in `runner.glb` is based on “Cesium Man” by Cesium (2017), from the Khronos Group glTF Sample Assets repository. It is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). The embedded Cesium mark was removed and the texture was modified for RIFTSYNC.

- Source: <https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CesiumMan>
- License: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- Changes: branded texture area replaced with a RIFTSYNC fracture insignia; start.ng appearance colors tint the model at runtime.
